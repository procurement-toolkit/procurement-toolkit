How to read the Incoterms 2020 responsibility chart

Cost split and risk transfer are two different things

Incoterms set both who performs and pays for each task, and who bears the risk of loss or damage. The two points do not always coincide. Under CFR, CIF, CPT and CIP the seller pays freight to the destination, yet risk passes to the buyer at origin. That is why a buyer may still need to arrange insurance for the main journey even though the seller paid the freight (except under CIF and CIP).

Three questions to settle before choosing a rule

First, the mode of transport. FAS, FOB, CFR and CIF are for sea and inland waterway only; for containerised cargo, FCA, CPT or CIP match the real hand-over point better. Second, who can clear customs. If the seller cannot clear imports or handle VAT in the buyer's country, DDP is not realistic. Third, insurance. CIF defaults to minimum cover (Institute Cargo Clauses C) and CIP to maximum cover (Clauses A), so two rules that both include insurance can protect very different things.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between FOB and CIF?

Who pays for freight and insurance. Under FOB the buyer arranges both; under CIF the seller pays freight and insurance to the destination port. Under both rules, risk passes to the buyer once the goods are on board the vessel.

How do DAP, DPU and DDP differ?

In all three the seller transports the goods to the destination. Under DAP they are delivered ready for unloading on the arriving vehicle; under DPU the seller also unloads. Under DDP the seller additionally pays import clearance, duty and taxes.

What happened to DAT in Incoterms 2020?

DAT became DPU (Delivered at Place Unloaded). The place of delivery is no longer limited to a terminal, and the seller unloads at that place.

Can I use FOB for container shipments?

You can, but it is not recommended. Containers are usually handed to the carrier at a terminal rather than on board, so the real hand-over and the point where risk passes can diverge. FCA is the better fit.
