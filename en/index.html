What you can do with these tools

CBM Lab collects the calculations and checklists a buyer or sourcing manager reaches for repeatedly, all free and without sign-up. They fall into a few working areas:

  • Cost and pricing. Work out the true delivered price with the landed cost calculator, compare ownership costs with the TCO calculator, and test a supplier's number with the should-cost model.
  • Logistics. Size a shipment with the CBM calculator and settle who pays for what with the Incoterms guide.
  • Sourcing strategy. Classify spend with the Kraljic matrix, then run the RFQ/RFP/RFI generator and score suppliers with the supplier scorecard.
  • Market context. Check indices and reference data in the market index hub.

Treat outputs as planning estimates and confirm against carrier, customs and supplier documents before committing money.

Frameworks referenced: Kraljic, P. (1983) Harvard Business Review 61(5); ICC Incoterms® 2020.
