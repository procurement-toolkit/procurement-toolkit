Korean import rules to check first

For broad shows the competent agency depends on the product. Fix the HS code first, check import requirements with Korea Customs Service and the agency responsible for that product (MFDS, KATS and others), then ask suppliers at the booth for the documents you need.

Official sources: Korea Customs Service · Korean Agency for Technology and Standards · FTA portal

Requirements vary by HS code and product detail; confirm the latest rules with the agency before you trade.
