Korean import rules to check first

Agricultural produce, seed, feed and farm inputs are subject to import risk analysis and quarantine by the Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency under the Plant Protection Act. Permission depends on product and origin country, so confirm that the item is importable from that country before you start talks.

Official sources: Animal and Plant Quarantine Agency · Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs · FTA portal

Requirements vary by HS code and product detail; confirm the latest rules with the agency before you trade.
