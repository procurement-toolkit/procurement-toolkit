Korean import rules to check first

Building and interior materials are checked against Korean Industrial Standards (KS), quality approvals for building materials and indoor-air criteria, depending on use. Fire performance for finishes and formaldehyde emission class for panels are common contract conditions.

Official sources: Korean Agency for Technology and Standards · Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology · FTA portal

Requirements vary by HS code and product detail; confirm the latest rules with the agency before you trade.
