Korean import rules to check first

Imported food and beverages to Korea fall under the Imported Food Safety Control Act, administered by the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS). Check business registration, import declaration and inspection status before you order, and ask suppliers at the booth for the ingredient list and label data you will need for Korean labeling.

Official sources: MFDS imported food portal · Ministry of Food and Drug Safety · FTA portal

Requirements vary by HS code and product detail; confirm the latest rules with the agency before you trade.
