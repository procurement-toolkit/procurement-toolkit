Assembly, handling, and automation trade fair.

Pre-visit checklist

  1. About 8 weeks before opening: get the exhibitor list, pick 5–10 suppliers to meet and request appointments.
  2. About 4 weeks before: book flights and hotel. Rooms in fair cities sell out early.
  3. Before departure: check import requirements (certification, labeling, customs) for the products you plan to buy.
  4. On site: note MOQ, quote validity, Incoterms, sample cost and certifications at every booth in the same format.
  5. Within a week of returning: send thank-you notes with quote and sample requests while the conversation is still fresh.

How to use a trade fair for sourcing

A question list beats a business card. Time at a booth is short. Asking MOQ, lead time, Incoterms, payment terms, certification and sample cost in the same order makes suppliers comparable.

Second day over first day. The first day is crowded. Revisiting key suppliers mid-fair often gets you the technical contact instead of a sales rep.

Convert quotes to landed cost. FOB prices from the floor only compare once freight, duty and VAT are added. Use the landed cost calculator to put every quote on the same basis.

Plan around local holidays. Replies slow down around local holidays before and after a fair. Check the global holiday calendar when planning the trip.
