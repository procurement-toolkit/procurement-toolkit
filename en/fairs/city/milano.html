How should I prepare before visiting an overseas trade fair?

About 8 weeks before opening: get the exhibitor list, pick 5–10 suppliers to meet and request appointments. About 4 weeks before: book flights and hotel. Rooms in fair cities sell out early. Before departure: check import requirements (certification, labeling, customs) for the products you plan to buy.
