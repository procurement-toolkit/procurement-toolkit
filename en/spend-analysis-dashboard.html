What Is Spend Analysis?

Why break spend down by category?

Looking at total spend alone only tells you whether it went up or down from last year. Breaking it down by category or supplier reveals where spend is concentrated (so you know where to focus negotiating power) and where supplier dependence is too high (a risk factor) — all at once.

How should I use this dashboard?

Start with your top 2-3 categories by spend share — review the target cost with the Should Cost Calculator, or consider a re-bid with the RFQ Comparison Tool. If a category has many line items, break it down further with the ABC Inventory Analysis Tool.

Frequently Asked Questions
How should I split categories?

There's no fixed answer. Common approaches split by nature (raw materials/components/MRO/services) or simply reuse the internal accounting chart of accounts. If you already have an ERP classification, reusing it directly is easiest.

Is a high share in one category always bad?

Not necessarily — core raw materials naturally carry a high share. A high share does mean a small negotiation improvement has a large dollar impact, which makes it a useful prioritization signal.

Should I also look at supplier concentration?

Yes — separately from category concentration, supplier concentration (dependence on one vendor) matters too. Even if no single category dominates, one supplier covering multiple categories can itself be a risk.
