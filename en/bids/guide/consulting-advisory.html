Consulting & advisory services: tender guide

Tenders for policy, technical and management advisory work and for supporting project design and delivery, usually bid by firms or teams.

At a glance

A two-stage process is common: an Expression of Interest first, then proposals only from shortlisted firms. Check whether the notice is an EOI or an RFP.

What notices appear in this sector

  • Notices seeking firms or teams for policy, technical and management advisory work and for supporting project design and delivery.
  • In development-bank and international-organization projects these are called technical assistance (TA), advisory services or project management consultants.
  • A two-stage approach is common: an Expression of Interest first, then proposals only from shortlisted firms.

Who buys

Government ministries, development-bank project units, international organizations and public bodies.

Finding notices by classification code

When a title does not make the sector obvious, classification codes help. European notices use the Common Procurement Vocabulary (CPV), while international organizations mostly use UNSPSC. The closest categories for this sector are:

73Research and development services and related consultancy
UNSPSC segments

What evaluators look at

  • Comparable advisory assignments and expertise.
  • Qualifications and experience of the key staff proposed.
  • Appropriateness of methodology, schedule and cost.

Common mistakes

  • Confusing an EOI with an RFP and missing required documents.
  • Omitting key staff availability confirmations.
  • Leaving deliverables from the terms of reference out of the proposal.

Document checklist

  • EOI letter with company profile and references
  • Key staff CVs
  • Technical proposal and methodology
  • Financial proposal

How a tender usually runs

  1. Find the notice — Check new notices daily and read the deadline, eligibility and submission method first.
  2. Eligibility and registration — Many buyers require registration on their e-procurement system or a platform such as UNGM. It takes time, so register early.
  3. Clarifications — Ask about unclear clauses in writing during the clarification period. Answers are shared with all bidders.
  4. Submission — Submit the technical and price proposals in the required format before the deadline, which may be in local time.
  5. Evaluation — Evaluation often goes eligibility, then technical, then price, and some notices do not award on price alone.
  6. Award and contract — After award notification the contract terms are agreed and signed. A guarantee may be required.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between EOI and RFP?

An EOI first checks interest and qualifications, while an RFP is the formal request sent to shortlisted firms.

What does TOR mean?

Terms of Reference, the description of the assignment.

Can we bid as a consortium?

If the notice allows it. Check the rules on associations.
