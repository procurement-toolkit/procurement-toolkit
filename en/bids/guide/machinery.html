Industrial machinery & plant: tender guide

Purchase and installation tenders for pumps, compressors, cranes, forklifts, construction machines, HVAC systems and elevators.

At a glance

Performance tests, acceptance testing after installation and spare-parts supply are common contract conditions.

What notices appear in this sector

  • Purchase and installation tenders for pumps, compressors, cranes, forklifts, excavators, boilers, HVAC systems and elevators.
  • Replacement and maintenance of plant equipment, construction equipment and power and cooling plant also appear.
  • Supply of machine parts such as sensors, measuring components and gaskets is included.

Who buys

Public facility managers, state construction and manufacturing companies, hospital and school facility departments and municipalities.

What evaluators look at

  • Compliance with performance specifications and safety standards.
  • Acceptance testing after installation and commissioning capability.
  • Spare-parts supply, service set-up and warranty.

Common mistakes

  • Not checking installation conditions such as power, piping and load.
  • Leaving acceptance-test costs and time out.
  • Not checking local safety-certification rules.

Document checklist

  • Equipment specifications and safety certificates
  • Performance test reports
  • Installation, commissioning and training plan
  • Spare parts and warranty terms

How a tender usually runs

  1. Find the notice — Check new notices daily and read the deadline, eligibility and submission method first.
  2. Eligibility and registration — Many buyers require registration on their e-procurement system or a platform such as UNGM. It takes time, so register early.
  3. Clarifications — Ask about unclear clauses in writing during the clarification period. Answers are shared with all bidders.
  4. Submission — Submit the technical and price proposals in the required format before the deadline, which may be in local time.
  5. Evaluation — Evaluation often goes eligibility, then technical, then price, and some notices do not award on price alone.
  6. Award and contract — After award notification the contract terms are agreed and signed. A guarantee may be required.

Some notices are supply-only and others include installation and commissioning.

How much spare-parts support is required?

The notice states warranty and spare-parts supply periods.

Are used machines tendered?

Most require new equipment. Check the notice.
